# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to get something right.

## What is expected

- Keep the conversation about the work: the code, the data, the measurement.
- Say what you measured and how. Disagreement about a technical claim is
  settled by running something, not by volume.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no to a change that is good work. The
  project is small and every feature is maintained for years.
- Assume good faith in a first misunderstanding, including across languages:
  this project is written in English by someone who does not speak it at home,
  and so are many of its issues.

## What is not

Harassment, insults, or personal attacks. Comments about a person rather than
their work, including about nationality, sex, gender, sexual orientation,
disability, appearance, race, age, religion or the language they write in.
Publishing anyone's private information. Sustained disruption of an issue or a
pull request.

## Scope

The issues, pull requests, discussions and releases of this repository, and
anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report it privately to the maintainer, through the contact on his GitHub
profile. Reports are read by him alone, and the person who reports is not named
to the person reported on without being asked first.

A report is answered, and the answer says what was done. The available
responses are a private word, a public correction, removing a comment, and
blocking an account from the repository. Which one is used depends on what
happened and on whether it happened again.
